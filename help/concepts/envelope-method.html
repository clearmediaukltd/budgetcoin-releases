    User guide

    The envelope method

    1. Money arrives in Ready to Assign.
    2. You assign it to categories, like putting cash into envelopes.
    3. Spending comes out of its category. If a category is overspent, move money from another one (see Overspending).
    4. Whatever is left in a category rolls into next month.

    You only budget money you already have.
