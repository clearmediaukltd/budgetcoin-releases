    User guide

    The first time you open BudgetCoin, it shows this agreement. Tick I have read and agree and choose Continue to start using it, or Quit to close the app. Your 45-day free trial starts when you agree.

    You can read it again at any time: Settings → Licence → View licence agreement, or on a Mac Help → Licence Agreement…. Both show the version you agreed to and when. You can select and copy the text. If a later update changes the agreement, the app shows the new version once and asks you to agree again. Your licence and budgets aren’t affected.

    The agreement is in English in every language version of the app.
