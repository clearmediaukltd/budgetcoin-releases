BudgetCoin User Guide

Entering transactions

Select an account in the sidebar to open its register: every transaction in that account, newest first, with the balance after each one.

Add a transaction

  1. Select the entry row at the top of the list (or press ⌘N, Ctrl+N on Windows and Linux). It’s the tinted row marked +, with an Add button at the end, and it’s always there, ready for the next transaction.
  2. Fill it in, using Tab to move between fields:
    • Date: type the digits in your budget’s date format, or choose the calendar button (Alt+↓). ↑/↓ move a day (see Entering dates).
    • Payee: who you paid or who paid you. Payees you’ve used before are suggested as you type, and picking one fills in the category you used last time (see Managing payees).
    • Category: which envelope the money comes from. For money coming in, choose one of the Income options so you can assign it on the budget:
      • Income for this month adds it to this month’s Ready to Assign straight away.
      • Income for next month holds it back until next month. Use it to live on last month’s income: pay that arrives late in November is budgeted in December, so you can fill December’s categories as soon as it lands without it showing up as unassigned money in November.
    • Memo (optional).
    • Outflow for money leaving the account, or Inflow for money coming in. Sums work (see Typing amounts and sums).
    • Cleared: select the round button if the transaction has already shown up on your bank statement.
  3. Press Enter (from any field, including the Category list) or choose Add.

The new transaction takes its place in the list below, by date, and is highlighted and tagged Added for a few seconds. If its date puts it further down, the list scrolls to it. If your search or filters hide it, a message tells you so.

The entry row empties, keeping the same date, ready for the next one. Clicking somewhere else also adds what you’ve typed. To start again, press Esc or choose ✕ to clear the row.

To spread one payment over several categories, choose Split into several categories… in the Category list. Each line gets its own category, memo and amount.

Transfers between accounts

To move money between two of your accounts, choose Transfer: account name as the category. The app adds the matching transaction to the other account automatically, and changing or deleting either side changes both. Transfers between two budget accounts don’t need a category, because the money stays in your budget.

Transfers to and from tracking accounts

Money that leaves your budget for a tracking account, such as a payment into a pension or a mortgage payment, comes out of a category. When you choose a transfer to or from a tracking account, a second list, Category in account name, appears below the row. Choose the budget category the money comes from, for example Retirement. Money coming back from a tracking account can go to a category, or to Income for this month or Income for next month so you can assign it again.

It works the same whichever account you enter it from. The budget account’s register shows the category; the tracking account’s register shows Transfer.

Tracking accounts

Transactions in tracking accounts (investments, property, loans) don’t use categories, because they aren’t part of your budget. Leave the category as No category, or choose a transfer.

Edit, delete and clear

  • Edit a transaction where it is: select its row (or move to it with ↑ and ↓ and press Enter). The row’s fields become editable. Press Enter or choose Save to save, or Esc to cancel. Clicking anywhere else, including another transaction, also saves your changes. If something’s missing, the row stays open and says what.
  • Delete removes it. Use Undo if you change your mind. If the transaction is reconciled (or it’s a transfer whose other side is), the app asks first, because deleting it means the account no longer agrees with your bank statement. This applies to Delete on selected transactions too.
  • The round button marks a transaction Cleared (it shows C) or not cleared. Reconciled transactions show R (see Reconciling an account).

Flags

Flags mark transactions you want to come back to, like “Tax” or “Refund due”. Select the flag button on a row and choose a colour, or Remove flag. You can also flag a transaction while you type it in the new-transaction row at the top: the flag is added with it. A flagged transaction shows its colour and name. To show only flagged transactions, one flag, or unflagged ones, use Filters in the search row (see Searching and filtering).

All accounts

All accounts in the sidebar lists every transaction from every account, newest first, with an Account column. The balance on each row is the balance of that row’s account. You can add transactions here too: choose the account first.

Change many transactions at once

Tick the box at the start of each transaction you want to change, or tick the box in the header to select every transaction shown (search or filter first to narrow the list; see Searching and filtering). A bar appears at the bottom of the register, over the list, so nothing moves. It has:

  • Mark cleared / Mark uncleared
  • Flag… to flag them all, or remove their flags
  • Change category… to move them all to one category
  • Delete

Each action changes all the selected transactions together, and one Undo reverses it. Some transactions can’t be changed this way and are left as they were: reconciled ones can’t be uncleared, and split, transfer and tracking-account transactions keep their categories. The app tells you how many were left.

Split a transaction across categories

One payment can cover several categories, like a supermarket shop with groceries and household items. In the Category list, choose Split into several categories…. Each line has its own category, memo and amount. Choose Add line for more, or Remove to drop one. The transaction’s total is the sum of its lines. A split can’t include a transfer.

Rename or close an account

Open the account, choose ⋯ (Account actions) at the top, then Rename. To stop using an account, bring its balance to zero, then choose Close account from the same menu. Closed accounts keep their history and are listed under Closed accounts in the sidebar, where you can open one and choose Reopen account.
