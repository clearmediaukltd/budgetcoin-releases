    User guide

    Searching and filtering transactions

    Every register, and All accounts, has a search row above the transactions. Use it to find a payment, check what you spent somewhere, or narrow the list before changing or exporting transactions.

    Search

    Type in the search box (or press ⌘F on a Mac, Ctrl+F on Windows and Linux, to jump to it). The list shrinks as you type. Search looks in:

    • the payee
    • the memo, including the memos on each line of a split
    • the category, including each category of a split
    • the account name, on All accounts

    Capitals and accents don’t matter: cafe finds Café. Matching text is highlighted in the list.

    You can also type an amount, the way your budget writes numbers (for example 50, 1,234.50, or 1.234,50 if your budget uses a comma for decimals). It finds that exact amount, money in or out, including the lines of a split. 50 finds 50.00 but not 5.00 or 150.00. A currency symbol or minus sign makes no difference.

    Press Esc in the search box, or the × at its end, to clear it.

    Filters

    Choose Filters to narrow the list further:

    • Date: This month, Last month, Last 3 months, This year, or Custom with your own from and to dates.
    • Status: not cleared, cleared, or reconciled.
    • Show transactions: flagged, one flag colour, not flagged, To approve, or Needs a category.

    The button shows how many filters are on, for example Filters · 2, and is tinted while any are. Reset filters in the panel turns them all off.

    Hide reconciled transactions

    Select the lock at the top of the cleared column to hide every reconciled transaction. While they’re hidden, the lock has a line through it and is tinted. Select it again to show them. Clear all leaves this setting as it is.

    A category from the budget

    When you choose Show in All accounts from a category’s Activity list on the budget, All accounts opens showing just that category’s transactions for that month: the date filter is set to the month, and a Category chip (for example Category: Groceries) appears next to Filters. Transactions on tracking accounts aren’t included, because they don’t count towards the budget. Select the × on the chip to show every category again, or Clear all to remove the month as well.

    What you see

    On the right of the search row, the count shows how many transactions are listed: 248 transactions, or 3 of 248 while a search or filter is on. Clear all removes the search and every filter.

    Searching and filtering only hide rows. They never change a balance: each row’s balance is still the account’s real running balance.

    Scheduled transactions waiting in the Scheduled panel aren’t searched. Ones that have already been entered are ordinary transactions and are.

    Act on the results

    • Change many at once: tick the box in the header to select every transaction showing, then use the bar that appears (see Entering transactions).
    • Export: Export to CSV saves the transactions showing to a spreadsheet file. While a search or filter is on it says, for example, Export 3 to CSV, and saves just those. The file is the same as the transactions export in Settings (see Exporting and backing up).
