    User guide

    Reports: spending and income

    Choose Reports in the sidebar to see where your money went and how much you kept. There are two reports, on two tabs: Spending and Income vs expense.

    Choosing the months

    Both reports cover whole months. Pick a Range: this month, last month, the last 3, 6 or 12 months (counting this month), this year, or last year. Choose Custom… to pick the first and last month yourself. The months covered are shown next to the range.

    Choose Chart or Table to switch between a chart and the same numbers as a table. The app remembers your choices until you quit.

    Spending

    Under Group by, choose Category or Payee.

    By category. A ring chart and a ranked list show how much you spent in each category group, largest first, with each group’s share of the total. Choose a group to see its categories, then a category to see which payees the money went to. The path at the top (for example All groups › Food › Groceries) takes you back up a level.

    By payee. The same chart and list show who you paid. The ten biggest payees get a slice each and the rest share one Other payees slice; choose it to list them. Choose a payee to see which categories its spending came from. Transactions without a payee are listed as No payee. Payees you’ve merged count as one.

    Point at a slice to see its exact amount. Either way:

    • Spending is net. A refund back into a category reduces what that category (and that payee) spent.
    • Net inflow. If more came in than went out (for example, a gift you booked to a category, or a shop that refunded more than you spent there), it can’t be a slice of the chart. It’s listed under the chart as net inflow instead.
    • Uncategorised spending (imported transactions you haven’t given a category yet) has a row of its own.
    • Hidden categories and hidden payees still count.

    The table view lists every group with its categories, or every payee with its categories.

    Income vs expense

    Totals for the months chosen (income, spending, net, and your savings rate, which is net as a share of income), then one pair of bars per month with net drawn as a line. The table view shows each month and a total row.

    • Income is money you put into Ready to Assign, including income held for next month. It counts in the month it arrived, not the month it’s budgeted in. A new account’s starting balance is income in the month you added the account, as it is in the budget.
    • Spending is the same net spending as on the Spending tab.

    What counts

    Only budget accounts count, and amounts are in your budget’s currency. Left out:

    • Transfers between your accounts, including paying off a credit card. That money was counted when you spent it.
    • A credit card’s starting debt, which was spent before you started the budget.
    • Tracking accounts.

    Saving a report

    Choose Export to CSV to save the report on screen for a spreadsheet:

    • Spending by category: one row per category, with its group, the amount spent and its share of all the spending in the chart.
    • Spending by payee: one row per payee and category, with the category’s group, the amount spent and its share of all the spending in the chart.
    • Income vs expense: one row per month and a total row.

    Reports can’t be saved as pictures or PDFs.
