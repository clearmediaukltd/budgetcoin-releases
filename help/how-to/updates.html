    User guide

    Updating BudgetCoin

    BudgetCoin checks once a day whether a newer version is out. When there is one, a notice appears at the top of the screen.

    Installing a new version

    1. Choose Download in the notice. Our website’s download page opens in your browser.
    2. Download the installer for your computer and run it. It installs over the version you have.
    3. Open BudgetCoin again.

    Your budgets are separate files, so installing a new version leaves them as they are. The app never downloads or installs anything by itself.

    If you don’t want a particular version, choose Skip this version. You won’t hear about it again, but you will about the next one. × hides the notice until the next check.

    New major versions

    Your licence covers every release of its major version: a BudgetCoin 1 licence covers 1.0, 1.1, 1.2 and so on (see Free trial and your licence). When the next major version comes out, the notice says so and links to the website, where you can see what’s new and the upgrade price. The version you have keeps working.

    If there’s also a newer release of the version you have, the notice offers that first.

    Checking yourself

    • On a Mac: BudgetCoin → Check for Updates…
    • Anywhere: Settings → Updates → Check now

    Settings → Updates also shows which version you have.

    Turning the check off

    Untick Check for updates automatically in Settings → Updates. The setting is for this computer and covers all your budgets.

    The check reads one small file from our website with the latest version numbers. Nothing about you, your licence or your budgets is sent. See Privacy & security.
