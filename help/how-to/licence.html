    User guide

    Free trial and your licence

    BudgetCoin comes with a 45-day free trial. Everything works during the trial: no features held back, no limits on budgets or accounts. The trial starts when you agree to the licence agreement, which the app shows the first time you open it.

    During the trial

    The bottom of the sidebar shows how many days are left. In the last week a reminder appears at the top of the screen, at 7 days, 3 days and on the last day. Close it with × and it stays closed until the next reminder. Reminders never appear during the setup tour.

    You can also see the trial in Settings → Licence.

    When the trial ends

    Your budgets stay yours. Without a licence you can still:

    • open, view and search every budget
    • see reports and net worth
    • assign money and move it between categories
    • edit, delete and reconcile the transactions you already have
    • export everything (JSON and CSV) and back up your files

    What stops is adding new transactions: the register’s entry row, importing bank files, new accounts with an opening balance, reconcile adjustments, and scheduled transactions being entered when they’re due (they wait and are entered as soon as you have a licence).

    Buying a licence

    Choose Buy in the sidebar, the reminder or Settings → Licence. After you pay, the licence key arrives by email, as text and as a .licence file.

    A licence is perpetual: the version you buy keeps working forever.

    • One licence covers all your own computers.
    • It includes every update of that version: a BudgetCoin 1 licence covers 1.0, 1.1, 1.2 and so on.
    • The next major version (BudgetCoin 2) is a paid upgrade. If you bought within 90 days before it came out, it’s included.

    Entering your licence

    1. Choose Enter licence (in the sidebar, in Settings → Licence, on the welcome screen, or in the app menu on a Mac).
    2. Paste the key from your email, or drop the .licence file onto the window (or use Choose file…).
    3. Choose Activate.

    The app unlocks straight away, with no restart, no internet connection and nothing changed in your budgets. Nothing is sent anywhere: the key is checked on your computer.

    If the key isn’t accepted, check that it was copied in full, from the first character to the last. Using the .licence file avoids copy mistakes. A key for an older major version is refused with a message saying which version it’s for.

    Your licence in Settings

    Settings → Licence shows who the licence belongs to, what it covers, the purchase date and the licence ID (quote it if you contact support). Remove licence from this computer… takes it off this computer, for example before you give the computer away. You can enter it again at any time; the key is in your purchase email.

    View licence agreement shows the licence agreement, with the version you agreed to and the date. On a Mac it’s also in Help → Licence Agreement….

    Privacy

    Your licence and trial live on this computer, in the app’s settings folder and your system keychain. They are never stored in your budget files and never sent over the internet.
