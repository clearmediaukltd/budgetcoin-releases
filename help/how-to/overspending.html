    User guide

    Overspending

    A category is overspent when more has gone out of it than it had. Its Available amount turns negative and orange.

    Fix it

    Assign more to the overspent category. Select its Assigned amount and type the extra, for example +50. If Ready to Assign is empty, take the money from another category the same way, with -50 on its Assigned amount.

    If you leave it

    At the start of the next month the category goes back to zero, and the overspent amount comes out of that month’s Ready to Assign. The month header shows it as Overspent in month. This works the same for spending on a credit card.

    Carry overspending forward

    Some categories are better left negative until you fill them, for example a reimbursable expense you’ll be paid back for, or a bill you pay ahead and budget for over the next few months. For these, keep the overspending in the category: in the month where it should start, select the arrow next to the category’s Available amount. The arrow shows when you point at the row or move to it with the keyboard. It saves straight away.

    From that month on, an overspent balance stays in the category and is carried into the next month as a negative amount. It doesn’t come out of Ready to Assign. Money you assign later fills it up first. The arrow stays shown, in orange, in every month where the category carries its overspending forward.

    Turning it on or off changes that month and every month after it. The setting stays on until you turn it off. To stop, point at the arrow in the month where it should stop: it turns into ✕. Select it. That month’s overspending then comes out of the next month’s Ready to Assign again. Undo reverses the change.
