    User guide

    Reconciling an account

    Reconciling checks that the app agrees with your bank, so small mistakes don’t build up. Do it whenever you get a statement, or every week or two.

    Before you start

    Open the account. At the top you’ll see three balances:

    • Cleared: transactions that have shown up at your bank (marked with C, or R once reconciled).
    • Uncleared: transactions you’ve entered that the bank hasn’t processed yet.
    • Working balance: the two together.

    Go through your bank’s list and use the round button on each matching transaction to mark it cleared.

    Reconcile

    1. Choose Reconcile.
    2. The app shows your cleared balance. If your bank shows the same amount, choose Yes, that’s what my bank shows. You’re done.
    3. If not, choose No, it’s different, enter the balance your bank shows and the statement date, and choose Check. For a credit card or loan, enter what you owe as a negative amount.
    4. If there’s still a difference, look for transactions you haven’t entered or haven’t marked cleared, and fix them. Or choose Add adjustment and finish to add a “Reconciliation adjustment” transaction for the difference. In a budget account the adjustment goes to Ready to Assign, so check your budget afterwards.

    When you finish, every cleared transaction becomes reconciled (R). Reconciled transactions are the part of your history you’ve confirmed against the bank. Undo reverses a reconcile if you need to. Deleting a reconciled transaction asks you to confirm first, since it would put the account out of step with the bank.

    Reconciled transactions are locked

    A reconciled transaction shows a lock beside its R. You can still change its memo (select the row and type) and its flag, but nothing else: not the date, payee, category, amount or account, so the account can’t drift away from the statement by accident. A transfer is locked when either side is reconciled. Bulk edits leave locked transactions as they are and say how many they skipped.

    To change one anyway, select its R and choose Unlock. It goes back to cleared, so your cleared balance doesn’t change, and you can edit it. The next reconcile locks it again. Undo reverses an unlock. For a transfer locked by its other side, unlock it in the other account.
