Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what the BudgetCoin app and this website collect, and why.

The app

Your budgets are stored as files on your computer. The app doesn't send your budget data to us or to anyone else.

It connects to the internet for two things, and both can be turned off in Settings:

  • Exchange rates, if you have accounts in other currencies: it downloads the European Central Bank's public rates file.
  • Update check, once a day: it reads the latest version number from getbudgetcoin.com.

These requests contain no budget data and no personal details. The server receiving them sees what any internet connection shows, such as your IP address.

There's no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting. Your licence is checked on your computer, without contacting us.

This website

This website uses no cookies and no analytics or tracking.

If you choose a language from the menu, your choice is saved in your browser so the site remembers it. The user guide saves its light or dark theme the same way. These settings stay in your browser.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitors' IP addresses to run and protect the service, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement

When you buy a licence

Payments are handled by Stripe, which collects your card details, name, email address and billing address. We don't see your card details. Stripe's checkout page uses its own cookies, for example to prevent fraud. Stripe's use of your data is covered by the Stripe Privacy Policy

We receive your name, email address, country and order details from Stripe. We use them to send your licence key, to send it again if you lose it, to answer your questions, and for our tax and accounting records.

Your licence key contains your name, your order number and a one-way code made from your email address, so it shows who it was issued to.

When you contact us

We use your message and email address to reply to you. The contact form is run by Formspree, which receives what you send and forwards it to us by email.

Who we share it with

Only the services that do the work described here: Stripe for payments, Formspree for the contact form, and the email service we use to send licence keys and replies.

How long we keep it

Purchase records are kept for as long as tax law requires, usually six years. Other messages are deleted when they're no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it for tax records. Depending on where you live, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If we change what the app or this website collects, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change.
