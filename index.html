How it works

The envelope method, on your computer.

Budget only the money you have today. When one envelope runs out, move money from another.

01

Count what you have

Money in your budget accounts that hasn't been given a job is Ready to Assign.

02

Give it a job

Assign it to categories: rent, groceries, the car, next summer's holiday.

03

Spend from the envelope

Each transaction comes out of its category. Overspend one and cover it from another.

Currencies

Accounts in any currency. One budget.

Your budget has one currency, and each account can have its own: a euro account, savings back home, a card in dollars. Balances show in the account's currency and count towards your budget at the rate you use.

Daily exchange rates

European Central Bank rates for about 30 currencies, downloaded once a day. Type your own rate for any other currency.

Each transaction keeps its rate

Past months don't change when rates move. Change a rate yourself if your bank used a different one.

Transfers between currencies

Enter what left one account and what arrived in the other. Ready to Assign stays the same.

Statements in any currency

Import a statement in the account's own currency. Each transaction is converted at your latest rate.

Questions

Before you start.

I used YNAB4. Will this feel familiar?

Yes. BudgetCoin uses the same method: you budget the money you have, month by month, and enter transactions yourself. It runs on your computer and is bought once.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. Enter transactions as you go, or download a statement file from your bank's website and import it.

Where are my budgets kept?

In files on your computer. Copy them like any other file to back them up, or export a budget from Settings.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. It goes online for two things: exchange rates, if you have accounts in other currencies, and a daily check for a new version. You can turn both off.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your budgets stay as they are. You can open, edit, assign money and export. Adding new transactions needs a licence.

What if I forget my budget's password?

Use the recovery key you saved when you set the password. Without the password or the recovery key, nobody can open the budget, including us.
